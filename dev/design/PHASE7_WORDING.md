# Phase 7: wording of methodology in the vignettes

The wording list of DEC-065, for the check-in after step 2 of the Phase 7 plan. It gives every sentence of the vignettes `pprof.Rmd`, `models.Rmd`, and `statistical-methods.Rmd` that states a statistical property, an interpretation, or guidance, with its source and its kind:

- **carried**: a statement of pprof 1.0.3's documentation that agrees with the code, kept (reworded where noted);
- **corrected**: a statement of pprof 1.0.3's documentation that contradicted the code, or overstated, rewritten to match it;
- **added**: a claim or guidance the documentation lacked (the project lead's answer to M-21, 2026-10-05), derived from the formulas the code computes or from the cited literature;
- **not carried**: a statement of pprof 1.0.3's documentation left out, with the reason.

Statements that only describe what the code computes (formulas, defaults, conventions, the behaviors awaiting sign-off stated as computed) are not listed; they are the specification and follow PROJECT_CONTEXT §5.7. Every reference was checked against Crossref or PubMed (2026-10-05). pprof 1.0.3's vignettes are cited by file name: `pprof.Rmd`, `Risk-adjustment_Model.Rmd`, `Logis-FE.Rmd`, `Linear-FE.Rmd`, and the help of `logis_firth()`.
