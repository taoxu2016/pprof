# CoxPH Phase C6: wording of the Cox documentation

> **Status: for the check-in of the C6 plan's decision 10 (DEC-139), 2026-10-16.** The project lead decides on the wording before the help pages, the developer guide, and NEWS are written in the same voice.

The wording list of DEC-065 as amended, for the Cox models: every sentence of the new and changed user documentation that states a statistical property, an interpretation, or guidance, with its source and its kind:

- **added**: a claim or guidance new to the documentation, derived from the formulas the code computes (cited by their `K-` entry, and shown in code where the vignette can), or established in the cited literature;
- **described**: a behavior awaiting a methodology owner's sign-off, or a decision of the project lead, stated as what the code computes, without calling it a defect or announcing a change (DEC-065 (1));
- **carried**: a statement of the existing vignettes, repeated for the Cox models.

Statements that only describe what the code computes (formulas, defaults, settings, the conventions of `dev/CONVENTIONS.md`) are not listed; they are the specification. pprof_py v0.7.0's documentation has no statements the package carries over. Every reference was checked against its source on 2026-10-16: Crossref's record of its DOI (Andersen and Gill 1982; Breslow 1974, whose last page is PubMed's; Efron 1977; Fine and Gray 1999; Garwood 1936; Lancaster 1961; Lin and Wei 1989; Lin, Wei, Yang and Ying 2000; Putter, Fiocco and Geskus 2007, whose issue Crossref dates to 2006, its online publication; Simon, Friedman, Hastie and Tibshirani 2011; Therneau and Grambsch 2000); He and Schaubel (2014) was checked in the rewrite's Phase 7.

Where: **T** is `vignettes/time-to-event.Rmd`, **S** `vignettes/statistical-methods.Rmd`, **M** `vignettes/models.Rmd`, **G** `vignettes/pprof.Rmd`, **R** the README and DESCRIPTION, with their sections.
